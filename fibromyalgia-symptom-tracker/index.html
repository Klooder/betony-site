Betony

A fibromyalgia tracker you can use on a flare day

Updated November 18, 2026

Fibromyalgia symptoms can suddenly improve or get worse, according to the NHS. That makes them hard to recall weeks later. Betony is designed for the days when typing hurts and focus is short: a full log takes under 30 seconds, with large tap targets.

What the fibromyalgia preset tracks

Pick Fibromyalgia during setup and Betony copies a starting list into your tracker. You can edit it at any time.

  • Symptoms: widespread pain, neck or shoulder pain, back pain, hip pain, morning stiffness, fibro fog, unrefreshing sleep, sensitivity to touch, fatigue, headache, numbness or tingling, stomach or IBS upset.
  • Numbers: sleep quality.
  • Triggers: weather change, stress, overexertion, poor sleep, cold, long sitting.

Every field is optional, and an untouched symptom is "not logged", not zero.

Seeing how weeks compare

  • Pain next to sleep quality on one chart.
  • A calendar of flare days and logged days.
  • Real highs and lows rather than averages.
  • Log yesterday if today you could not. No streaks, no guilt.

Medications and reports

Log medications with optional reminders. Betony Pro adds a doctor report and longer trends, built only from your own entries.
