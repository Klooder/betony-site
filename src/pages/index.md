# Betony

Betony is a symptom tracker built for chronic illness — POTS, endometriosis, fibromyalgia, lupus, ME/CFS, and everything the world calls "invisible".

A full day takes under 30 seconds to log. Big buttons, no typing, nothing mandatory. Your data stays on your iPhone: no account, no sign-up.

[Download Betony on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/betony-symptom-tracker/id6813134795) — free, with an optional Pro upgrade.
