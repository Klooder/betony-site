# An ME/CFS tracker that costs as little energy as possible

With ME/CFS, logging itself uses energy. Betony is built around that: a full day takes under 30 seconds, with large buttons, no typing and nothing required. Days you could not log can be filled in later or left empty.

## What the ME/CFS preset tracks
Pick ME/CFS during setup and Betony copies a starting list into your tracker. You can edit it at any time.
- **Symptoms:** PEM (crash after exertion), exhaustion, brain fog, dizziness when upright, muscle pain, joint pain, unrefreshing sleep, light or sound sensitivity, sore throat, tender lymph nodes, headache, feeling feverish or chills.
- **Numbers:** morning energy.
- **Triggers:** physical exertion, mental exertion, social exertion, poor sleep, being upright too long, stress.

Physical, mental and social exertion are separate, so each can be logged on the day it happened.

## Seeing crashes in context
Post-exertional malaise can follow activity after a delay and last for weeks, according to the NHS. Betony's month calendar puts exertion days and crash days side by side, and charts show the real highs and lows of each symptom.
- **Compare** a symptom with morning energy.
- **Flare days** marked on the calendar.
- **No streaks** and no messages about missed days.

## For appointments
Betony Pro builds a report from your entries: top symptoms, flare days and medications, with no conclusions added.
