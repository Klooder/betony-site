# An endometriosis tracker for pain, bleeding and cycle day

Endometriosis symptoms can follow the cycle or appear at any time, and diagnosis often takes years. The NHS suggests giving your GP as much information about your symptoms as you can. Betony keeps that information in one place and takes under 30 seconds a day.

## What the endometriosis preset tracks
Pick Endometriosis during setup and Betony copies a starting list into your tracker. You can change it at any time.
- **Symptoms:** pelvic pain, period pain, heavy bleeding, spotting between periods, endo belly or bloating, ovulation pain, fatigue, lower back pain, leg or hip pain, nausea, pain during intimacy, pain with urination, pain with bowel movements, constipation or diarrhea.
- **Numbers:** cycle day and bleeding level.
- **Triggers:** menstrual cycle, ovulation, stress, certain foods, physical activity, poor sleep, long sitting.

Nothing is mandatory. A symptom you skip is "not logged", never "no pain".

## Seeing the pattern across cycles
- **Compare any symptom with cycle day**, for example pelvic pain across several cycles.
- **A month calendar** marks flare days and logged days.
- **Honest charts** show the worst days as they were, not smoothed into an average.
- **Backfill** a day you could not log. Missed days carry no warnings.
