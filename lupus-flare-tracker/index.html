A lupus flare tracker

Updated November 18, 2026

Lupus often moves between flares and calmer periods, and the NHS notes that the reason symptoms flare up or settle down is not known. A daily record shows when each flare started, how long it lasted and what was logged around it. In Betony, a full day takes under 30 seconds.

What the lupus preset tracks

Pick Lupus (SLE) during setup and Betony copies a starting list into your tracker. You can edit it at any time.

  • Symptoms: joint pain or swelling, rash, butterfly rash, sun sensitivity, fatigue, low-grade fever, mouth sores, hair loss, Raynaud's (cold fingers), brain fog, chest pain on breathing, headache.
  • Numbers: temperature.
  • Triggers: sun exposure, stress, infection or illness, cold, poor sleep.

Skip anything. A skipped symptom is "not logged", never "none".

Marking flares

  • Flare days appear on the month calendar.
  • Charts show each symptom's real highs and lows.
  • Compare a symptom with temperature or any other number you log.
  • Backfill the days you could not log during a flare.

For check-ups

Lupus usually means regular check-ups. Betony Pro turns your entries into a short report: top symptoms, flare days and the medication log, with no interpretation added.
