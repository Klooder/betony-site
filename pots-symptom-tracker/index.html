Betony

A POTS symptom tracker that fits a bad day

Updated November 18, 2026

POTS symptoms change from day to day, and many of them come and go with standing up. A record that covers weeks makes the pattern easier to describe at an appointment than memory alone. Betony is built so that a full day takes under 30 seconds to log, with big buttons and no typing.

What the POTS preset tracks

When you pick POTS / Dysautonomia during setup, Betony copies a starting list into your tracker. You can change it at any time.

  • Symptoms: dizziness when standing, lightheaded or near-faint, fainting, racing heart, palpitations, brain fog, fatigue, heat intolerance, nausea, shakiness, chest discomfort, headache.
  • Numbers: heart rate lying, heart rate standing, salt intake and fluid intake.
  • Triggers: heat, prolonged standing, a large meal, dehydration, the menstrual cycle, stress, poor sleep, overexertion, alcohol.

Every field can be skipped. A symptom you leave alone counts as "not logged", not as zero.

Seeing the month

  • A calendar shows logged days, flare days and gaps at a glance.
  • Charts keep the real highs and lows instead of averaging them away.
  • Comparisons put a symptom next to any number you log, such as dizziness next to fluid intake.
  • Missed days can be filled in later. There are no streaks and no reminders about gaps.

Medications and appointments

Medications can be logged with optional reminders. Betony Pro turns your entries into a report for your doctor: top symptoms, flare days and the medication log, built from what you recorded, with nothing interpreted.

Private by design

No account and no sign-up. Everything stays on your iPhone, and a CSV export is free. Read the privacy policy.
