A symptom diary built for flare days.

Betony is a symptom tracker for chronic illness: POTS, endometriosis, fibromyalgia, lupus, ME/CFS. A full day takes under 30 seconds to log, with big buttons and no typing.

Download on the App StoreFree on iPhone. Optional Pro upgrade.

Under 30 seconds

Tap a number for each symptom and you are done. Nothing has to be typed.

Nothing is mandatory

Skip any field. A symptom you leave alone counts as not logged, never as zero.

Stays on your iPhone

No account, no sign-up, no analytics. Export everything to CSV for free.

Log the day, see the month, take a report to your doctor

Set up for your condition

Pick a condition during setup and Betony fills in symptoms, numbers and triggers that people with it tend to track. Change any of it later.
