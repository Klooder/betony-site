Betony Privacy Policy

Effective: October 2, 2026. Betony is developed by Ivan Borovets, sole proprietor (FOP), Kyiv, Ukraine ("we").

The short version: your data stays on your device. We don't collect anything.

What Betony stores

Symptoms, metrics, medications, notes, and settings you log are stored only on your device. Your device's own backups (such as iCloud Backup) may include Betony's data under Apple's terms — we have no access to it.

What we collect

Nothing. Betony has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no servers. We cannot see, access, sell, or share your health data — it never reaches us.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple. We receive anonymized transaction information from Apple (what was purchased, when) and no payment details.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device and never leave it.

Your data, your control

Export everything to CSV at any time from Settings. Deleting the app deletes all locally stored data (and iCloud copies can be removed via your device's iCloud settings).

Children

Betony is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version will always be at betonyapp.com/privacy with an updated date.
